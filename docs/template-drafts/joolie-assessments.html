How scoring works

Each scored question is shown as Yes / No buttons. The answer marked 1 point if Yes or 1 point if No adds one point; the total is the number of risk factors or hazards found. Questions marked not scored are recorded only.

Open items

  • Vulnerability: the packet does not say what "Exit Risk Factor?" means; it is recorded per factor but not scored.
  • Neither assessment defines risk levels, so completed results show "Score: N" (N = risk factors or hazards found). Named risk levels need agreed cut-offs and a small code change.
  • These assessments do not generate care-plan tasks automatically (the generator only recognises Katz ADL, Lawton IADL, PHQ-9 and Mini-Cog).

At JOOLIE HEALTHCARE SERVICES, LLC., we understand that experiencing a fall can be a life-changing event That’s why we’ve developed our “Focus on fall prevention” program, a unique approach that pairs our clinical expertise with patient education for our patient and family to reduce fall risk by helping to address common causes of falls among our patients, including environmental hazards both inside and outside the home. This Home Safety Checklist will guide you through key environmental considerations to help and increase home safety and reduce the likelihood of falls in your homes or that of a loved one.
