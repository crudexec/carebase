Patient Bill of Rights

AS OUR CLIENT, YOU HAVE THE RIGHT TO EXERCISE YOUR RIGHTS, AND/OR TO DESIGNATE A REPRESENTATIVE TO EXERCISE THEM FOR YOU:

  • Choose a health care provider and be informed of provider service / care limitations
  • Be informed of any financial benefits when referred to an organization.
  • Be informed of anticipated outcomes of service/care and of any barriers in outcome achievement.
  • Be treated with respect, and full recognition of your dignity and without regards to age, race, gender, sexual orientation, national origin, disability, religion, economic status, political beliefs or diagnoses

  • To expect all communications and medical records pertaining to your care be treated as confidential.
  • Be informed of the process to voice concerns regarding care or lack of respect to property and/or to your person without being subjected to discrimination or reprisal.
  • Be entitled to know the name of responsible person directly involved in coordination and supervising your home health care and the manner in which that person may be contacted during regular working hours.
  • To refuse any segment of treatment to the extent permitted by the law without relinquishing other segments of the treatment plan. You have the right to be informed of the medical consequences of your action.
  • To the extent that is reasonably possible to be involved in the planning and implementation of the Home Health plan of care and its expected outcome.
  • To be informed of the policy and procedure for registering formal complaint about the services being provided. *You have the right to be informed that the Home Health services will not be disrupted as a result of filing a complaint.
  • To expect that within its capacity the agency must make a reasonable response to your request for services.
  • To receive copy of your bill from your agency, and request explanation of charges regardless of source of payment
  • To know what agency rules and expectations apply to your conduct as a client.
  • To be involved in the planning and implementation of home health plan of care and to request copy of your plan of care
  • Be informed of your right to formulate an Advance Directive and/or Do Not Resuscitate (DNR) order.
  • To be informed, both orally and in writing, in advance of services/care to be provided, including charges of the payment for service/care expected from third parties and any charges for which you will be responsible.
  • To have the pain evaluated and its intensity controlled. Be free of physical and mental abuse/neglect and/or exploitation. Be informed of the availability of the JOOLIE HEALTHCARE SERVICES, LLC consumer line to make a complaint regarding any Home Health Agency or in the implementation of the Advance Directives at (240) 206-8767 from 9am to 5pm and to report Abuse, Neglect, or exploitation: (240) 206-8767, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
  • To have property treated with respect.
  • To voice grievance regarding treatment or care that is (or fails to be) furnished, or regarding lack of respect for property by anyone who is furnishing services on behalf of the Agency, and must not be subject to discrimination or reprisal for doing so.

As a Home Health Care patient, you have the responsibility to:

  • Give accurate and complete health information concerning your past illness, hospitalization, medications, allergies, and other pertinent items.

5. Grievance Procedure Acknowledgment

Agency grievance procedures, timelines and appeal process.

Grievance procedures

  1. Any person, who believes he or she has been subjected to discrimination, or otherwise denied equitable and fair treatment, may file a grievance under these procedures. The Agency will not retaliate against anyone solely for filing a grievance or cooperation in the investigation of a grievance.
  2. Grievances must be submitted to the Agency within thirty (30) days of the date the person filing the grievance becomes aware of the action.
  3. A complaint should be in writing/phone, containing the name and address of the person filing it. The complaint must state the problem or action alleged to have occurred and the remedy or relief sought by the grievant.
  4. The Director of Nursing or Administrator shall conduct an investigation of the complaint to determine its validity. This investigation may be informal, but it has to be thorough, affording all interested persons the ability to submit evidence relevant to the complaint.
  5. The Director of Nursing will maintain the files and records of the Agency relating to such grievance.
  6. The Director of Nursing will issue a written decision on the grievance no later than thirty (30) days after its filing.
  7. The grievant may appeal the decision of the Director of Nursing by filing an appeal in writing to the Administrator of JOOLIE HEALTHCARE SERVICES, LLC within fifteen (15) days of receiving the Director of Patient Service decision.
  8. The Administrator shall issue a written decision in response to the appeal no later than thirty (30) days after filing.
  9. The availability and use of the grievance procedure does not preclude a person pursuing other remedies accorded by local, State and Federal Laws and Regulations.
